## Here's the problem.

1. When someone searches "biryani near me", Google looks at your website to find your menu.
2. Most restaurant websites hide the menu inside code Google can't read. Google sees an empty page and shows someone else.
3. So people order from the delivery apps instead. The apps take a cut of every order, and they keep the customer. You never learn their name.

## How it works

1. **We build your website.** Your name, your menu, your own web address. Every dish written so Google can read it. We do the work. You check it and say yes.
2. **Orders print in your kitchen.** Online orders go straight into your Clover, or to a printer if you don't have one. A text message backs up every order so nothing gets lost.
3. **You keep the money.** Payments land in your bank account, not ours. No monthly bill. No contract. Leave whenever you want.
