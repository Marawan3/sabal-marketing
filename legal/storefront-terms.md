# Orders

Prices and availability can change. An order is accepted when the restaurant confirms it (it appears as a paid ticket for the kitchen), not merely when you tap place-order. Pickup times are estimates. Delivery, when offered, is performed by a delivery-network partner (DoorDash Drive in the current integration).

# Payment

You pay the restaurant. Cards are entered in the payment processor’s hosted fields (NMI). The restaurant and Sabal do not receive your full card number. Chargebacks follow the card network and the restaurant’s merchant account.

# Phone number and SMS

You must enter a phone number to order. Sabal uses that number as the contact for the order and, when SMS is configured, to send **transactional** messages about this order (confirmation and, if the restaurant cancels, cancellation).
